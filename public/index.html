Notes on the numbers

One reference, two price measures.

Retail prices are the lowest “from” offers shown by Gold.de’s bar comparison. The fine-gold spot quote is captured from Gold.de’s market-price page. Legacy 20g, 50g, and 100g retail history is preserved; spot was not recorded by the earlier tracker, so no past spot values are invented.

A personal price reference only. Not investment advice or a guaranteed checkout price.
